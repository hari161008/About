Permissions We Request & Why

Ever Dialer is a full-featured telephony replacement application. To fulfill this role effectively as your default dialer, the app requests standard Android system permissions:

Permission Why It Is Required
READ_CONTACTS
WRITE_CONTACTS		 Used to display caller names on incoming/outgoing calls, search contacts from the T9 dialpad, and allow creating or editing contact details locally.
CALL_PHONE
MANAGE_OWN_CALLS		 Used to place outbound telephone calls when you dial a number or tap a contact.
ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS Allows answering incoming phone calls directly using Ever Dialer's custom in-call interface or gesture modes (such as Raise to Answer).
READ_CALL_LOG
WRITE_CALL_LOG		 Enables displaying your recent call history, call duration, missed call counts, and deleting call history entries when requested by you.
READ_SMS
SEND_SMS
RECEIVE_SMS		 Used solely for the dedicated Ever SMS messenger tab to view conversational threads, send texts, and receive incoming messages when configured as an SMS handler.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
...PHONE_CALL		 Keeps ongoing calls alive reliably in the background, updates the in-call notification controller, and handles call-time gestures (Raise to Answer / Rain Mode).
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays heads-up notifications for incoming calls, missed calls, and new SMS messages.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Used optionally to display a floating in-call bubble or mini-overlay when you navigate to other apps while a phone call remains active.

Call Recording Policy

Google Play Flavor: In full compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, audio recording features are completely disabled and excluded from the Google Play Store build of Ever Dialer. The RECORD_AUDIO permission is removed.

Open-Source / GitHub Flavor: In builds where call recording is available, all audio captures are stored strictly on your local device storage inside the directory specified by you. Recordings are never sent across the network or accessed by anyone other than the device owner.

Disclaimer: Users are solely responsible for complying with all applicable local, state, and international laws regarding recording telephone conversations without prior consent.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

Ever Dialer is committed to a clean, non-commercial user experience:

  • No Ads: We do not integrate Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or any other mobile advertising networks.
  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not embed Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Flurry, Mixpanel, or Crashlytics.
  • No Tracking: There are no device fingerprints, advertising IDs (GAID), or hardware serials gathered.
  • External Links: If you tap voluntary external links (e.g., our GitHub repository, developer website, or Telegram support group), your interaction with those external sites is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Data Retention & User Control

Because we do not store any user information on servers:

  • You retain complete ownership and sovereignty over your device's data at all times.
  • All app settings, dialpad preferences, contact notes, and theme selections are saved in your phone's private app storage.
  • You can immediately wipe all data associated with Ever Dialer at any moment by navigating to Android Settings > Apps > Ever Dialer > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

Ever Dialer is a general-purpose communications tool and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect any personal information from children. Since all operations remain strictly local to the user's handset, no child data is ever transmitted or processed remotely.
